# Hi, I’m Brooks

I’m a senior software engineer who works directly with clients and owns projects from discovery through production. I turn business needs into technical plans, design the architecture, build across the stack, and carry the work through launch and ongoing support.

I build custom web applications, client portals, APIs, and integrations using **PHP, Laravel, Ruby, Python, JavaScript, React, and WordPress**. My work includes building new systems and modernizing applications that businesses already depend on.

## What I bring to a project

- **End-to-end ownership.** I run discovery, scope the work, design the system, build the frontend and backend, and handle deployment and production troubleshooting.
- **Architecture and modernization.** I’ve redesigned workflows, migrated data and documents between systems, and taken changes through staging and production.
- **Integrations and automation.** I connect applications to third-party APIs and build workflows that move data between the systems people use every day.
- **Direct client collaboration.** I work with stakeholders to clarify requirements, make tradeoffs explicit, and decide what to ship first. I explain technical decisions in terms of what they mean for the business.
